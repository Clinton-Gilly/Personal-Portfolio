01

About

I turn business problems into software that people actually log into every day.

Most of what I build lives in the gap between a spreadsheet and a real system — a SACCO tracking member savings on paper, a landlord chasing rent over SMS, a support team drowning in the same five questions. I take that, design the flow, and ship a web app that handles it end to end.

My default stack is Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase and Postgres, deployed on Vercel. I integrate M-Pesa STK push and card payments because that's what the market here runs on, and I've been building increasingly with LLMs and RAG — support desks, receptionists and knowledge-base assistants that answer from a company's own content.

I care about the boring parts too: role-based access, audit trails, exports the treasurer can take into a committee meeting. A pretty interface that loses data isn't a product.
